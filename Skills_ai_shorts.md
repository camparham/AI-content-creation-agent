# SKILL: AI Instructional Videos

Use this skill for any AI-focused video content request — scripts, descriptions, titles, thumbnails.

---

## Teaching Methodology

- Always frame the real-world use case before introducing any AI concept or tool
- Show practical, low-barrier examples — not hype or abstract theory
- One concept or tool per video — never overload beginners
- Anchor every concept to a data or business outcome
- Write for the person who's heard of AI but hasn't used it yet

---

## Agent Clarifying Questions

Before writing any script or description, ask:

1. What is the primary keyword for this video?
2. What AI tool or concept is being covered? (Claude, ChatGPT, prompt engineering, agents, etc.)
3. Who is the target audience? (beginner / intermediate / data professional)
4. What platform is this for? (YouTube / TikTok / LinkedIn)
5. What's the specific pain point or use case this video solves?
6. How does this connect to data thinking or the analyst skill set?

---

## Script Format (60 seconds / ~150 words)

| Section | Timing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **HOOK** | First 1–3 seconds | Stop the scroll. Lead with a result, a bold claim, or a problem the viewer recognizes. |
| **VALUE BODY** | Middle | Show the use case first, then the AI concept. Practical > theoretical. |
| **CTA** | End | Specific and actionable. Tell viewers what to do and why. Tease the next video. |

**Closing line (required):**
> "I'm Cam, your upskilling and reskilling coach, don't forget to subscribe for more AI and data help!"

## Title Formula

- Lead with the AI tool, concept, or outcome
- Include a number when possible (3 prompts, 1 trick, 5 use cases)
- End with audience signal (for beginners, every analyst needs, no coding required)
- Always include "AI" in the title
- Under 70 characters for mobile readability

**Examples:**
- "3 AI Prompts Every Data Analyst Needs in 2025"
- "How I Use AI to Analyze Data in 60 Seconds"
- "AI for Beginners — Do This Before You Write a Single Prompt"

---

## Thumbnail Text Rules

- Max 5 words
- Must be readable at mobile size
- Include a number or AI tool name when possible
- Best performer format: *"3 AI Prompts That Actually Work"*

## Viral Research Instructions

When researching content for a topic:

1. Find the top 5 viral YouTube videos on the topic
2. Analyze: title structure, keywords, first 30 seconds of description, hook patterns, CTA style
3. Identify common patterns across titles and hooks
4. Apply those patterns to Cam's script and description
5. Flag which keywords are trending vs. saturated
6. ⚠️ Analyze patterns only — never copy titles, descriptions, or scripts verbatim
